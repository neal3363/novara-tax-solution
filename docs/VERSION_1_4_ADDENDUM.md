- Maximum 100 external partners

- Target $10,000 monthly partner subscription  
- NOVARA First Look before external release  
- Exclusive opportunity assignments  
- Anti-circumvention controls  
- 60% NOVARA / 40% partner only when legally authorized for the specific transaction

## 6. Preview honesty rule

Synthetic or mocked capabilities must be labeled. Preview functionality must not be represented as production-ready or as live official-source coverage.

## 7. AI authority (restated)

AI may recommend and assist but cannot make final legal determinations of ownership, heirship, title, or entitlement.

## 8. Amendment precedence

Where this addendum conflicts with v1.3 on competitive UI scope or preview sequencing, v1.4 may accelerate interface delivery. Where it conflicts on legal, data-rights, security, compensation, or partner isolation, **v1.3 controls**.
