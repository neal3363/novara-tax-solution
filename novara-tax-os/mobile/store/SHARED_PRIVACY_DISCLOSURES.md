# Mobile privacy disclosures (draft — counsel must approve)

NOVARA Tax OS processes tax preparation information including identity details, document images, and payment-related data necessary to provide the service.

## Collection

- Account contact information  
- Tax documents you upload  
- Answers provided in the interview  
- Device identifiers for session security and (when enabled) push delivery  
- Payment metadata via Stripe or Apple/Google billing when activated  

## Not collected for sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not use sensitive tax data for third-party advertising.

## Retention & deletion

Users may request account deletion from account settings. App Store and Play require an accessible deletion path. Retention of filed returns follows legal recordkeeping requirements to be finalized with counsel.

## Security

Encryption in transit; production encryption at rest via cloud KMS when production infra is authorized. Browser-local storage is not the system of record for SSNs or tax documents.

## Live features

Live IRS transmission, live banking settlement, and live software charges remain disabled until security testing, IRS authorization, and owner approval complete.

**This draft does not replace counsel-approved Privacy Policy / Terms.**
